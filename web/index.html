THE FIELD GUIDE

Curiosity, with context.

Choose a scenario, adjust a bounded teaching parameter, then run. Scrub or play the timeline. Curves always represent the applied experiment—not unsubmitted controls.

Try reduced cooling, a source step, or source-off. Observe delay and stored heat without treating these as predictions for a nuclear facility.

What is—and is not—modeled

  • Two perfectly mixed thermal nodes, a finite source lag and linear heat transfer. Invented coefficients; all values dimensionless.
  • Exact source relaxation and implicit Euler heat balance at step 0.1. Integer-time output. First-order discretization error remains.
  • No fuel types, rod configurations, neutron kinetics, criticality, decay heat, boiling, radiation, pressure, equipment failures or real-plant limits.
  • No live monitoring, hardware control, nuclear safety claims or certification. This cannot assess a real reactor.
